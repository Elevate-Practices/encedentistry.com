The Importance of Regular Dental Check-Ups

October 12, 2013

Book online(435) 628-2667

Children can start regular routine dental exams can be done when they reach 6 months of age. It is just as important to get your baby’s gums, jaw and few teeth examined and cleaned as it is for older children and adults. Starting your regular dental checkups at an early age is a great habit for your kids to develop. It is recommended to have your regular dental check-up, which includes; mouth, teeth, jaw exam and teeth cleaning every six months, if not once a year. Once a year your dentist will take X-rays of your teeth and to find any early signs of tooth decay, wisdom teeth, gum disease and many other dental issues. Regular dental exams will help you avoid any unwanted issues in the future. If there are any problems with your gums or teeth it is best to detect them early on. Early detection can save you lots of money in the future and also save you the pain of a true dental crisis. Your dentist can go over proper at-home dental care with you. Early dental care prevents any other dental issues or diseases. Small dental issues if left untreated can lead to more extreme situations such as extractions or root canals.

By going for your regular dental check-up, your dentist at St. George, Utah can discuss your brushing habits with you. He will know whether you are brushing too little or too much. Is your toothbrush too hard or not hard enough? Maybe you are flossing incorrectly and it’s not helping your gums. Not flossing enough? Your dentist will cover all of these things and more with you at your regular dental check-up. It is important for you to have good dental hygiene, which leads to an overall good bill of health. Your dentist can also help you with any tooth discolorations you may be unsatisfied with you, cosmetic dentistry, bad breath, dental fillings, etc. A dental checkup can also show signs of non-dental conditions, such as vitamin deficiencies and even HIV.

Most people do not visit the dentist for fear of pain. After years of not visiting the dentist the fear is that the pain will stack up. This situation is easily avoidable by visiting your dentist at St. George, Utah and having your routine exam done twice a year. You will be left with a beautiful healthy smile.
