Wendy

Gerente de la oficina

Wendy is the Office Manager at Ence Dentistry. She brings over 20 years of experience in the dental field. For nearly nine years here in our practice, she's helped patients feel informed, comfortable, and confident in their dental care while building lasting relationships with them and their families. Outside of work, she enjoys biking, boating, and spending time with her grandchildren.

Angela Ence Ayala

Higienista dental

At 15, Angela started helping her dad at the office, and she kept at it until she went to dental hygiene school. She graduated from the Dixie State dental hygiene program in 2007 and worked full time until 2011, then went part time after having kids. She and her husband, Alan, have three children. Angela loves spending time with family and friends, going to the lake, swimming, sports, and watching her kids grow and develop. She enjoys meeting patients and getting to know them all while cleaning their teeth.

Ashley Conover

She loves to hike, fish, and go to Lake Powell and Joe's Valley with family and friends. Ashley is always up for an adventure.

Amy

Outside of dentistry, she enjoys hobby bee keeping, hiking, canyoneering and travel. She also loves spending time with her family.
