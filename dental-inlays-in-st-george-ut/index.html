What an inlay is

An inlay is a custom piece made to fit the tooth exactly, then bonded in place. It's used when a cavity or old filling is too big for a regular filling, but most of the tooth is still healthy.

Why choose one

  • Keeps more of your natural tooth than a crown
  • Made to fit your tooth exactly
  • Strong for chewing on back teeth
  • Tooth-colored options

How it works

  1. We remove the decay or old filling
  2. We take an impression of the tooth
  3. You wear a temporary while the inlay is made
  4. We bond the inlay in place and check your bite

Common questions

Inlay or crown: which do I need?

It depends on how much healthy tooth is left. The doctor will show you on your X-rays and photos and explain the options.

How long do inlays last?

With good brushing, flossing and checkups, many last for years.
