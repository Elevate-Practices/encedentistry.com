Zoom teeth whitening in St. George, UT

Professional whitening for a brighter smile, safely.

Ence Dentistry offers Zoom whitening at our office on Main Street in St. George, Utah, for patients from all over Southern Utah. Call (435) 628-2667 or book online.

Why professional whitening

Coffee, soda and time can dull your smile. Professional whitening is stronger than store kits and done with your teeth and gums in mind.

How Zoom whitening works

  1. We check that your teeth and gums are healthy
  2. We protect your gums and lips
  3. A whitening gel is applied and activated with a special light
  4. You see the difference the same day

Keep it bright

Rinse after coffee, tea or soda, and keep your regular cleanings. Some people get mild sensitivity for a day or two.

Common questions

Will whitening make my teeth sensitive?

Some people feel mild sensitivity for a day or two. We'll help you keep it comfortable.

Does whitening work on crowns or fillings?

No. Whitening only lightens natural teeth. We'll plan around any crowns or fillings.
