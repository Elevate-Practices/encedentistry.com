Dental fillings in St. George, UT

Fix a cavity before it grows, with a filling that strengthens the tooth.

Book online(435) 628-2667

Ence Dentistry offers fillings at our office on Main Street in St. George, Utah, for patients from all over Southern Utah. Call (435) 628-2667 or book online.

When you need a filling

A cavity is a small hole where decay has worn through the enamel. Caught early, it's fixed with a simple filling. Left alone, it can grow and need a bigger repair.

How it works

  1. We numb the tooth so you're comfortable
  2. We remove the decay
  3. We fill and shape the tooth
  4. We check your bite and polish

After your visit

Your cheek may feel numb for a couple of hours. Wait to eat until the numbness wears off, and call us if your bite feels high.

Common questions

Will it hurt?

We numb the area first, and patients often say they hardly feel a thing. Nitrous oxide is available if you're nervous.

Most fillings take under an hour.
