Nitrous oxide (laughing gas) in St. George, UT

A safe, gentle way to relax during your visit.

Ence Dentistry offers nitrous oxide at our office on Main Street in St. George, Utah, for patients from all over Southern Utah. Call (435) 628-2667 or book online.

What nitrous oxide is

Nitrous oxide, often called laughing gas, is a mild sedative you breathe through a small mask over your nose. It helps you feel calm while you stay awake and can talk to us.

Who it helps

  • Patients who feel nervous about the dentist
  • Longer visits
  • People with a strong gag reflex
  • Kids and adults

After your visit

The effects wear off within minutes after the mask comes off, so most people can drive themselves home.

Ways to pay

We're in network with many dental plans and will help you use your benefits. We also offer an extended payment plan with interest-free options, and CareCredit.

Common questions

Will I be asleep?

No. You stay awake and relaxed, and you can talk to us the whole time.

Is nitrous oxide safe?

Yes. It's been used in dentistry for many years, and we watch you closely the whole time.

Can kids have it?

Often, yes. We'll talk with you about whether it's right for your child.
