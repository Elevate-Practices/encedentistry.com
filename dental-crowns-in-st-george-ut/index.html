Protect and restore a cracked, worn or weakened tooth.

When a crown helps

A crown is a cap that covers the whole tooth. It's used for a tooth with a large filling, a crack, heavy wear, or after a root canal, so you can chew comfortably again.

How it works

  1. We numb the tooth and shape it
  2. We take an impression
  3. You wear a temporary crown
  4. We place your custom crown and check your bite

Caring for a crown

Brush and floss like normal and keep your regular cleanings. A crown can last many years.

Common questions

Does getting a crown hurt?

We numb the tooth first, so most people feel pressure rather than pain.

How long does a crown last?

With good care, many crowns last 10 years or more.
