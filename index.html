Questions we hear a lot

Are you taking new patients?

Yes. We see kids, teens and adults. Book online or call (435) 628-2667.

Do you take my insurance?

We're in network with many dental plans. Call us and we'll check yours and help you get the most from your benefits.

I don't have insurance. What can I do?

Join the Ence Membership: two cleanings, exams and X-rays each year, plus 20% off other care, starting at $375 a year.

I'm in pain. How fast can you see me?

Call us at (435) 628-2667. We'll work to get you in as soon as we can and tell you what to do in the meantime.

What are your hours?

Monday through Thursday, 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM. Fridays are by appointment.
