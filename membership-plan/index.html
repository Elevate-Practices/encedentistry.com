Traditional dental insurance premiums are expensive, and benefits are limited on most plans. As an Ence Member, your preventive care (cleanings, X-rays and exams) is 100% covered in our office, and you save on restorative care like fillings, crowns and implants, and even cosmetic dentistry.

Common questions

Is the membership insurance?

No. It's an annual reduced-fee savings plan with our office. No claims, deductibles, yearly maximums or waiting periods.

Can existing patients join?

Yes. It's open to new and existing patients.

Who is the Dual plan for?

Two people: a parent and child, or a husband and wife.

How does the Family plan work?

It's $360 per family member each year, for children until age 19.

How do I join?

Call (435) 628-2667 or ask at your next visit.
